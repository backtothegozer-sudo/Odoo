Odoo 19 loyalty and promotions: reward customers without eroding margin

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a promotion should not become a parallel price that nobody can explain. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, discount and loyalty programmes connect Sales, eCommerce and Point of Sale: they can attract, reward and accelerate a purchase, provided that the rule, channel and cost remain controlled.

Choose the mechanism before configuration

Odoo includes coupons, loyalty cards, promotions, discount codes, Buy X Get Y offers and next-order coupons. The decision is not merely a marketing label. Define the purpose: one-off acquisition, higher basket value, repeat purchase, stock clearance or long-term reward. A pricelist answers a structured pricing need; a loyalty programme better fits a public, conditional or time-sensitive offer.

Write a commercially testable rule

Before creating the programme, define eligible products or categories, the minimum amount or quantity, tax treatment, validity period, usage limit and whether already-discounted products are excluded. Odoo can grant points per order, currency spent or unit paid, then offer a discount, free product or free shipping. Avoid a rule that customer, sales staff and finance will interpret differently.

Separate channels and companies

Across Odoo Belgium, Odoo France and multi-company environments, the same campaign does not automatically mean the same rule. Limit the programme to the appropriate company, website, point of sale or pricelist when needed. Check currency, products sold in each country and the roles allowed to change the programme. A promotion available everywhere by default can cause margin leakage long before it appears in reporting.

Prevent unintended stacking

The usual risk is not one visible discount but several benefits combined: pricelist, code, reward, eWallet, credit note or manual gesture. Decide which combinations are permitted and who may make an exception. In Odoo Enterprise, governance should bring together Sales, eCommerce, Point of Sale and Finance: the same basket must lead to a consistent result across every authorised channel.

Acceptance testing that also measures margin

Fictional Underside scenario: a Belgian and French retailer launches a loyalty offer to reward repeat purchases without confusing it with B2B pricing. The team tests a basket just below the threshold, one at the threshold, a partial return and an online order collected in store. It validates the customer price, commercial cost and expected accounting treatment.

  • Test start and end dates, usage limits and the customer-facing message.
  • Verify products, categories and minimum amounts with tax included or excluded according to the written policy.
  • Compare an eCommerce purchase and Point of Sale transaction for authorised channels only.
  • Test earning, redemption and expiry of points, plus a single-use coupon.
  • Check stacking with pricelists, gift cards, credit notes and manual discounts before launch.
  • Track redemption, incremental revenue, reward cost and margin, then decide whether to continue or adjust the offer.
