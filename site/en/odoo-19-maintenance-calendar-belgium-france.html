Odoo 19 Maintenance Calendar: plan downtime without disrupting the workshop

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a maintenance calendar should make downtime explicit before it becomes a production delay. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, it brings corrective and preventive requests together. Its value is not simply selecting a date: it is arbitrating between equipment availability, work-center capacity and execution ownership.

Schedule a request with actionable context

From the calendar, a request can be created and then completed with its equipment or work center, team, responsible person, scheduled date, duration and priority. Keep the manufacturing-order link when maintenance concerns a specific production run. These elements give the workshop actionable information; a vague title and an isolated date do not.

Distinguish breakdowns, prevention and unavailability

A corrective request handles an already observed issue; a preventive request reserves an intervention intended to reduce risk. Keep both signals separate so a workload indicator does not conceal real breakdowns. For a work center, Odoo provides an option that blocks the planning of work orders and other maintenance during the selected duration. Use it when downtime is genuinely required, after agreement with production.

Make the calendar a shared decision

Calendar view does not replace a capacity review. Before approving a time slot, the team should know what must move, who informs affected people and what condition permits return to service. List, pivot, graph and activity views can then help follow open requests, overdue work and workload by technician or equipment.

Plan closure that explains variance

An intervention is not complete just because its slot has elapsed. Add instructions, a worksheet or suitable evidence: a measurement, photograph, replaced part, passed check or issue to continue investigating. When an emergency moves preventive work, retain the reason for the postponement and a newly planned decision. That is how a maintained calendar differs from a merely populated one.

Acceptance-test conflicts before go-live

Fictional Underside scenario: a Belgian industrial site schedules two hours of maintenance on a packaging work center. The day before, a corrective incident occurs on the same center. The team tests the work-center block, the impact on work orders, urgency priority, documented postponement of preventive work and evidence of return to service.

  • Create a corrective and a preventive request from the calendar.
  • Test one request for equipment and another for a work center.
  • Verify the team, responsible person, duration, priority and company.
  • Test a work-center block with the affected production team.
  • Simulate a conflict between an emergency and a preventive slot.
  • Check closure evidence and follow-up of the postponement.

Official source and scope

Official Odoo 19 documentation on the Maintenance Calendar describes creation, scheduling, filters and views for corrective and preventive requests. Governance rules and acceptance scenarios are Underside’s analysis; verify access rights, safety procedures and production constraints in your own environment.
