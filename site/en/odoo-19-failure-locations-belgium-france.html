Odoo 19 Failure Locations: isolate nonconformities without distorting inventory

A nonconforming product that remains available creates a misleading stock promise. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a failure location is a practical handover between quality control, warehouse, purchasing and manufacturing. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, it helps isolate units that fail a check; the next step is to give every affected unit a decision, an owner and evidence.

What the location makes visible

Odoo lets a failure location be associated with a quality control point. When a check fails, products can be moved there so they are no longer mixed with usable stock. Choose an identifiable physical area and an unambiguous label: “under review”, “vendor return” and “scrap” do not necessarily express the same decision.

Configuration should not turn every alert into an automatic verdict. A measurement deviation, damaged packaging or traceability issue may each require a different treatment. Define the information needed: lot or serial number, quantity, source, failed check, possible photograph and decision deadline.

Connect the check to the right operation

A control point can target a particular operation, such as a receipt, manufacturing or delivery. For your Odoo Belgium or Odoo France project, start with one genuine risk flow: receipt of a critical component or final inspection of a finished product. Avoid multiplying checks whose result nobody uses; a useful rule is one that leads to repeatable action.

Also document the expected effect on available and reserved quantities. Sales and planning teams need to know whether a moved unit remains sellable, can be brought back into conformity or requires a replacement order. Test this reading in your actual configuration and installed modules.

Decide before the isolation zone ages

Assign someone to review failed units daily. Quality qualifies the issue; purchasing or manufacturing then approves, as appropriate, a vendor return, repair, rework, scrap or release back to stock. Retain the reason for the decision and the link to the document that carries it out.

A simple dashboard is often enough: quantity and value immobilised, age of units, most common causes and pending decisions. Do not equate isolated quantity with a final loss: the indicator should distinguish products under review from those with an approved outcome.

Acceptance-test the decision, not only the move

Fictional Underside scenario: while receiving a lot for a French SME, an out-of-tolerance measurement fails. The team verifies that the affected units reach the failure location, the lot is retained, available stock reflects the movement and a person receives the analysis request. It then tests an authorised release back to stock and a vendor return, confirming that each option leaves an understandable trail.

  • Create a control point for a representative operation and product.
  • Fail the check for a partial quantity of a lot.
  • Check the target location, lot, quantities and access rights.
  • Verify the impact on availability before accepting a new order.
  • Test release, vendor-return and scrap decisions under your rules.
  • Measure the elapsed time from failure to analysis and final decision.

Official source and scope

Official Odoo 19 documentation on failure locations describes configuring them with quality control points. Responsibilities, thresholds and acceptance scenarios are Underside’s analysis: adapt them to your traceability obligations, warehouse and quality procedures.
