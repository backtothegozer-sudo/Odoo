Odoo 19 Work Orders: reliable workshop execution

A routing only creates value when every operator knows what to do, where to do it and when to report an exception. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, Odoo 19 Enterprise work orders connect manufacturing orders to operations, work centers and actual time spent.

Start with a routing people understand

Break production into operations with observable outcomes: cut, assemble, inspect and pack. Each operation should name a work center and include a clear instruction. Avoid generic labels that hide inspection steps, tooling changes or handovers between teams.

Make time data useful

Expected time should support decisions. Separate preparation, execution and inspection, then compare declared time with a sample of completed orders. Qualify the reason for a variance: setup, waiting for material, quality rework or work-center unavailability.

Sequence operations without losing exceptions

Dependencies can block an operation until a previous step is completed. They are useful for curing, mandatory inspection or first-article approval. Define the normal case and who can authorise an exception.

Acceptance-test an end-to-end flow

Underside fictional scenario: workshops in Namur and Lyon assemble the same product through three operations. The team launches a normal order, one with a missing component and one requiring rework. It checks instructions, sequence, time and variance handling.

  • Test opening, starting and completing every work order.
  • Verify instructions, documents and units at the relevant work center.
  • Simulate a blocked operation and validate the exception rule.
  • Compare expected time, declared time and variance reason.
  • Check partial production, scrap and rework against quality rules.

Official source and scope

Odoo 19 documentation on work centers explains how to enable work orders. Instruction, exception and acceptance-testing rules are Underside recommendations to adapt to your workshop.
