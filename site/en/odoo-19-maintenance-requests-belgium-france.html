Odoo 19 maintenance requests: prioritise without losing context

A failure may be urgent without being well qualified. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a useful maintenance request states what is affected, why it matters and who should act. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, a request links equipment or a work center, intervention type, team and owner; the challenge is to make these fields consistent with how your organisation makes decisions.

Describe the problem before classifying it

Start with a title that makes the situation understandable without opening the record: equipment, symptom and area. In the notes, add when it was observed, the effect on operations, safety measures already taken and, where useful, a photo or reference. Avoid titles such as “urgent”: urgency is a decision, not a description.

Select the right affected object. A request linked to a machine feeds its history; one linked to a work center provides capacity and scheduling context. This choice affects search, metrics and the people who will have to explain the incident later.

Make priority a service rule

Odoo priority stars order requests on the Kanban board. They do not independently define a guaranteed response time. We recommend connecting them to a simple rule: people risk, a critical-flow stoppage, available workaround and customer impact. Also decide who can raise a priority and who arbitrates when two critical requests arrive at once.

Keep a normal priority for improvements and irritants that do not stop work. Otherwise, the board fills with emergencies and no longer distinguishes a failure from a comfort request.

Assign a team, then an owner

The maintenance team owns the flow; the owner identifies the person managing the request. Do not assign a placeholder technician to make a card disappear from the board. When no one is available, the request should retain a team, a visible status and a date to review.

In a multi-company environment, check the request company and real access rights. Someone allowed to report an incident does not necessarily need to edit every group asset. This distinction reduces unnecessary access without blocking field reporting.

Schedule without hiding downtime impact

Use planned date and duration as working assumptions to test against the schedule. For a work center, the blocking option can prevent other work or maintenance from being planned during the intervention. Test it with your manufacturing orders: it does not replace a production decision or communication with the workshop.

Link the manufacturing order or work order when it explains the incident. You avoid having to reconstruct later which batch, serial range or sequence was affected.

Acceptance-test the full cycle, not only creation

Fictional Underside scenario: a Liège company notices abnormal noise on an end-of-line sealer. An operator opens a corrective request with the equipment, symptom, time and shipment impact. The maintenance lead assigns the team, sets priority under the agreed rule, schedules the work, then closes the request with diagnosis and actual time spent. The team finally checks that asset history and metrics remain understandable.

  • Create a corrective request on equipment and a preventive one on a work center.
  • Verify visibility with operator, technician and manager access rights.
  • Test your priority rule with two competing incidents.
  • Check work-center blocking on a test schedule.
  • Move the request through real stages to closure or disposal.
  • Review notes, duration and history with the team that will use the board.

Official source and scope

Official Odoo 19 documentation on maintenance requests describes available fields, priorities, stages and access rights. The service rules, arbitration and acceptance scenario are Underside recommendations to adapt to your organisation.
