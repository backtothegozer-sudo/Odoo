Odoo 19 SMS Marketing: send useful messages without losing control

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, SMS is an immediate channel, but it should not turn into expensive outreach that is difficult to justify. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, SMS Marketing lets teams prepare, schedule and analyse mailings; the operational task is to connect each message with consent, a controlled audience and a measurable outcome.

Start with the use case and lawful basis

An appointment reminder, delivery information and a re-engagement campaign do not deserve the same preparation. Before configuration, define the purpose, audience, business owner, data used for segmentation and process for handling opt-outs. Odoo Belgium and Odoo France teams should document their lawful basis and retention rules with their compliance owners: configuration does not replace legal analysis.

Protect list quality in Odoo

A mailing list is only as reliable as its contacts. Standardise phone-number format, avoid duplicates and retain an understandable record of preferences. Odoo provides SMS mailing lists and blacklists; make them a process control rather than a technical afterthought. An excluded contact must remain excluded when an import, CRM sync or Odoo migration runs.

Write a message that also controls cost

With Odoo’s IAP service, credits are prepaid and cost depends in particular on destination and message length. Odoo documentation distinguishes GSM7 and Unicode: special characters, including ones commonly used in French text, can switch a message to Unicode and reduce the available length per segment. Write the final copy in Odoo, check the displayed counter, then test it on the phones your teams actually use.

Handle links as conversion points

An SMS should lead to an identifiable action: confirm an appointment, view an order, register for an event or request a quotation. Prepare a mobile-appropriate landing page, trackable link and success measure for the scenario. In Odoo Enterprise, then relate clicks to opportunities, quotations or revenue carefully: useful attribution supports a marketing decision; it does not automatically turn correlation into a sale.

Acceptance-test before the first high-volume send

Fictional Underside scenario: an SME operating in Belgium and France wants to re-engage inactive customers after reviewing communication preferences. It sends an internal test, checks segments and exclusions, then compares a short version with one containing a link. The campaign only goes live after its estimated cost, rendering and operational handling of generated replies or requests are approved.

  • Check phone numbers, duplicates, blacklists and segmentation rules on a controlled sample.
  • Test a message with accented characters, special characters and a link to verify the billed segment count.
  • Check destination, available credits and access rights for users who may schedule or send messages.
  • Verify mobile rendering, the linked page and the expected action after a click.
  • Keep evidence of the campaign purpose, audience and company-approved opt-out process.
  • Track deliveries, clicks, leads and business outcome, then refine frequency rather than multiply sends.
