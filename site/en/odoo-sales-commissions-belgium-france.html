Odoo 19 sales commissions: govern incentives without distorting payroll

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a commission plan is not merely a formula. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, it connects sales targets, commercial data and management approval: an ambiguous rule can quickly become a remuneration dispute or a misleading margin indicator.

Start with a written policy, not an Odoo field

Before configuration, decide what earns a commission: a confirmed order, an invoice, margin, quantity sold or new monthly recurring revenue. Also define cancellations, credit notes, unpaid invoices, shared sales, salesperson changes and attribution periods. Odoo calculates according to the selected plan; it does not replace an employment agreement or the checks required from HR, finance and employment advisers.

Choose targets or achievements

Target-based plans reward progress towards a fixed goal and can include levels. Achievement-based plans apply a rate to a concrete metric. Odoo 19 can track sold amount or quantity, invoiced amount or quantity, margin and, with the Subscriptions app, new MRR. Pick a metric that the team understands and your data produces consistently.

Match data to the intended behaviour

A commission on sold amount encourages signing; one on invoiced amount is closer to the billing cycle; one on margin assumes costs are maintained. These are management choices, not equivalents. For Odoo Belgium and Odoo France, do not mix local rules, exchange rates or undocumented exceptions in one plan. When several companies use the same database, identify the entity, currency and owner for every rule.

Lock the plan and organise exceptions

In Odoo, an approved plan cannot be edited directly: it must return to draft. This constraint is useful when backed by a clear process: who proposes a change, who checks outcomes, who approves it and how the team is informed. Do not silently rewrite history to process an exception. Retain an explicit decision, the affected period and the originating sales document.

Acceptance testing for Odoo Enterprise

Fictional Underside scenario: a Brussels services business sells projects and subscriptions to Belgian and French customers. It plans invoice-based commission for projects and a new-MRR metric for subscriptions. Before launch, sales management, finance and HR check that every result can be explained from an order, invoice and approved rule.

  • Create a target-based plan with monthly targets, then verify levels at 0%, 100% and above 100%.
  • Create an achievement-based plan and compare sold amount, invoiced amount and margin with test data.
  • Test a cancelled invoice, credit note, shared sale and salesperson change according to your written policy.
  • Check access to plans, results and approval for sales, finance and HR roles.
  • Export a sample of results, have the calculation approved, then document the transfer to payroll without assuming that a sales calculation is a payslip.

Official source and scope
