Odoo 20: digitize vendor bills without automating errors

Digitization does not magically turn a bill into a reliable accounting entry. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, it primarily speeds up document intake, data extraction and purchase matching. In Odoo 20 Enterprise, OCR can create and pre-fill a vendor bill from a PDF or scan; accounting quality still depends on suppliers, purchase orders and approval rules.

Choose the right intake point

A bill can be dropped into the purchase journal or sent to the journal’s email alias. Odoo can also collect documents in the Documents app through an alias. Make the choice match the real process: one address for a shared supplier inbox, or controlled upload for sensitive documents. For structured electronic documents, XML is preferable to an image: Odoo documentation states that it is processed without OCR credits.

Before opening the flow, define accepted formats, naming conventions, people allowed to submit documents and duplicate handling. A collection address without an owner quickly becomes a second unmanaged mailbox.

Make the purchase order the reference

After recognition, Odoo searches for a matching purchase order. Matching is not a decorative bill field: it lets teams compare lines, quantities and prices against what was ordered and received. Discrepancies should be visible and assigned to purchasing, receiving or accounting before posting.

Plan a separate route for bills without a purchase order. It can be justified for rent or professional fees, but it must not become the usual bypass for purchasing policy. The same rule applies to an Odoo Belgium SME and an Odoo France subsidiary: automation reduces data entry, not accountability.

Control automation and IAP credits

Odoo states that digitizing scanned or PDF documents is an In-App Purchase (IAP) service using one credit per document. Measure actual volume, incoming formats and correction rates before scaling OCR. Automatic posting for selected vendors should be enabled only after a controlled period and for a stable scope.

Keep human approval when the supplier, tax, amount, currency, bank details or due date is outside expectations. This matters especially during an Odoo migration, when supplier history and open purchase orders have just been loaded.

Acceptance-test from document to bank reconciliation

Underside fictional scenario: a business operating in Brussels and Lille processes a PDF bill with a purchase order, an XML bill and a bill without an order. The team checks the source document, pre-filled fields, matching, price exception and posted status. It then verifies that due date and payment reference support correct bank reconciliation.

  • Test a clear PDF, an imperfect scan, an XML file and a multi-line bill.
  • Verify supplier, reference, dates, tax, currency, due date and bank details.
  • Simulate a quantity or price discrepancy against a partially received purchase order.
  • Define who corrects, who approves and who may enable automatic posting.
  • Track IAP credits, exceptions and the share of bills matched without correction.
  • Test permissions by company, journal and team in a multi-company database.

Official sources and scope

Official Odoo 20 documentation on document digitization describes OCR, aliases, purchase matching and IAP credits; the vendor-bill documentation details purchase-order matching.
