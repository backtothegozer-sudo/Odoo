Odoo 19 quotation templates: sell faster without making offers generic

A recurring quotation should not make a sales team start from a blank page. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, quotation templates make offers consistent while keeping salespeople responsible for qualifying the actual need. With Odoo 19 Enterprise, the objective is not to freeze the sale: it is to reuse what is stable and make what changes visible.

Decide what deserves a template

Create a template for a genuinely repeatable offer: a service contract, implementation package, training service or known product configuration. Start with the commercial promise and the boundaries of the offer, not with the available fields. A clear name, the appropriate company in a multi-company environment and a suitable validity period prevent a local template from becoming the default for the entire group.

A template does not replace pricing or approval. Keep decisions that depend on the deal outside it: exceptional scope, deal-specific discount, committed delivery date or negotiated clause.

Structure lines so customers can understand them

In Odoo, template lines can be organised with sections and explanatory notes. Use them to separate preparation, delivery, training and follow-up, so customers read a proposal rather than a raw catalogue. Check quantities, units and pricelists against the data used in each country or company.

Plan the variable-product scenario too. An optional product can support cross-selling, but it must not conceal the cost of the base offer. Add only options that the team can explain in terms of value, price and operational impact.

Frame signature, payment and terms

Depending on the features enabled in your database, a template can propose online signature, online payment or both. These mechanisms change the confirmation journey, so test them with your providers, approval rules and contractual information. Do not enable an option merely because it is visible on the screen.

The template's terms and notes should be reviewed by the appropriate teams. Odoo makes them reusable, but it does not decide their commercial scope or their validity for Belgium, France or a particular market.

Keep room for commercial adaptation

The best template reduces repetitive entry without creating identical offers for different situations. Ask salespeople to check the customer, delivery and invoicing addresses, pricelist, taxes, payment terms and expiry before sending. A prefilled offer remains a draft quotation until it has passed the agreed approval process.

Track frequent adaptations. If a line is removed from most quotations, the template is probably too broad. If the same lines are added manually, create a focused variation instead of one universal template that is difficult to maintain.

Test from opportunity to sales order

Fictional Underside scenario: a Brussels services business prepares an implementation offer for a French customer. The salesperson creates a quotation from the opportunity, applies the appropriate template, checks company, pricelist, sections and terms, then adds an optional training item. They preview the portal offer and confirm that the option does not change the total until the customer selects it. The team then confirms the test quote and checks the sales order, invoicing and access rights.

  • Create a template for a frequent offer and a variant only for a truly distinct case.
  • Test lines, sections, notes, quantities, prices and taxes with a test pricelist.
  • Check optional products or sections in the customer portal.
  • Test the validity period, terms and confirmation email.
  • If enabled, test signature and payment in a non-production scenario.
  • Confirm a test quotation and check the information passed to the sales order and invoice.
