Odoo 19 Social Marketing: manage a multichannel campaign

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, posting on social networks does not by itself make a campaign. With Odoo 19 Enterprise, Social Marketing can centralise business accounts, content and metrics; its value mainly comes from a clear scope, identified responsibilities and measurement connected to business objectives.

Connect authorised accounts to the right scope

Before creating content, inventory the business pages and accounts that the company actually administers. Odoo documents the addition of streams for several platforms and notes that personal profiles are not the appropriate integration point. For organisations operating in Belgium and France, assign each account to the relevant company, appoint an owner and verify the permissions granted by the platform.

Make the campaign a management object

An Odoo campaign needs a testable objective: a launch, event registration, demand generation or customer information. Give it a stable name, useful tags and a business owner. Campaigns can bring together social posts, email mailings, SMS messages and push notifications when the required apps and settings are available. This structure avoids comparing messages that have no relationship to each other.

Prepare content and calendar

An effective post connects a promise, audience, landing page and expected action. Prepare the copy in the audience’s language: accurate French for French-speaking audiences in Belgium and France, and English for the relevant audiences. Verify the rendering for each network, images, links and publication time. Odoo scheduling helps teams view drafts, scheduled content and published content, but it does not replace editorial approval.

Measure without over-interpreting

Social interactions are signals, not automatic evidence of revenue. In Odoo Enterprise, connect the campaign to quotations, leads or revenue when the journey allows it, then document the attribution rule being used. Compare outcomes by objective, channel and period; do not combine awareness and conversion campaigns in the same performance review.

Acceptance-test access and the journey before publishing

Fictional Underside scenario: an SME launches a campaign in Belgium and France. It connects business accounts, schedules posts adapted to each audience and adds a follow-up email to the same campaign. Before launch, it checks access rights, previews on every channel, links, the destination page and the data that will feed reporting.

  • List business accounts, their company, their administrators and active permissions.
  • Name the campaign, objective, owner, tags and business metric to track.
  • Validate French and English content separately, including links, visuals and calls to action.
  • Check previews, scheduled dates and time zones before any publication.
  • Test the journey from the post to the form, quotation, registration or target page.
  • Review results at campaign end and keep the resulting decisions for the next one.

Official sources and scope

References: Odoo 19 documentation on Social Marketing, social marketing campaigns and social posts. It describes accounts, streams, campaigns, posts and metrics. Content choices, audiences and attribution rules remain governed by your company; the implementation recommendations are Underside’s analysis.
