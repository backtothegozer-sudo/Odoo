Odoo 19 Click & Collect: keep the online stock promise

Click & Collect is more than adding a pickup button at checkout. For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, it connects a promise made online to a warehouse, stock and a team able to honour it. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, that promise becomes dependable when collection locations, availability and the in-store journey are configured as one operating flow.

Make the collection location a real operational unit

In Odoo, in-store pickup is configured as a delivery method linked to one or more warehouses. Start by naming collection locations as customers recognise them, then check that each has a complete address. The address also enables correct display in the selector: an incomplete warehouse can therefore disappear from the customer journey even though stock is not the issue.

Then define who prepares, checks and hands over the order. A counter, back room or shop are not interchangeable: the stock shown online must match the place where the customer will be welcomed.

Do not promise an item the customer cannot collect

Odoo’s documentation states that the location selector is displayed when the product is in stock; when the out-of-stock sales option is enabled, the outcome can differ. That option is a commercial decision, not a remedy for uncertain stock data. Before enabling it, decide what the customer sees, which lead time can genuinely be met and who handles the exception.

Check quantities, reservations and lead times for high-demand products. An item available in global stock but not at the chosen collection point creates disappointment that a good confirmation email rarely solves.

Test the multi-company boundary

In a group, Odoo requires the warehouse company to match the company of the website using the pickup method. The rule prevents silent flow mixing, but it deserves a concrete test: selling company, price, taxes, documents, stock and the access rights of the team preparing the order.

For an organisation operating in Belgium and France, keep a readable rule for what belongs to the website, selling company and physical location. If the commercial promise changes by country, warehouse or language, separate configuration can be safer than a manual exception at the counter.

Prepare the journey from basket to counter

Click & Collect creates a chain: web order, preparation, ready-for-collection status, notification and handover. Odoo structures the sales and delivery stages; the organisation must decide how to identify the customer, where to place the ready order and how to handle a partial collection, delay or no-show.

Do not confuse collection with an internal delivery. A transfer to a shop may be necessary, but the customer should only receive a confirmation when the order is actually available according to your service rule.

Acceptance-test the promise, not only the setting

Underside fictional scenario: an SME sells from Brussels and Lille using Odoo eCommerce. It configures two collection locations, enters their addresses, then orders an item available in Brussels and unavailable in Lille. The team checks basket options, selling company, preparation, notification and customer handover. It then repeats the test with an out-of-stock sale and a multi-company order to confirm that exceptions follow a known rule.

  • Test an available item, an unavailable item and, where applicable, an item allowed to be sold out of stock.
  • Verify the address, opening hours and labels for every collection location.
  • Check that the stock offered matches the location selected by the customer.
  • Replay preparation, notification and handover with the real access rights of the teams.
  • Test company, taxes and documents for every relevant website and warehouse.
  • Document the handling of late, partial or cancelled collections.

Official source and scope

The official Odoo 19 documentation on delivery methods and Click & Collect describes activation, warehouses, product availability and the multi-company constraint. The promise, preparation and acceptance rules are Underside recommendations to adapt to your business.
