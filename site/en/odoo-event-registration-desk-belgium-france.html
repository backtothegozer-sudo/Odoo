Odoo 19 Event Registration Desk: reliable event check-in

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, check-in is when a registration becomes verified attendance. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, the Event Registration Desk can search for an attendee or scan a badge; its real value comes from a clear process shared by event, sales and welcome teams.

Make attendance trustworthy data

A registrant is not necessarily an attendee. Define the status that confirms attendance: an entry scan, a manual check-in by the welcome desk, or a documented exception process. Every team and time slot must use the same rule, otherwise attendance KPIs, follow-ups and CRM data become inconsistent.

Prepare badges and identifiers

Odoo documents lookup by name, email or badge number, as well as badge scanning. Before the event, check attendee-data quality, the printed format and code readability. A badge should be more than decoration: connect it to the event, ticket type and expected attendee to avoid namesakes and incorrect validation.

Design a check-in flow that keeps working

A registration desk needs to stay easy to use under pressure. Assign appropriate access rights to welcome staff, prepare devices and test the venue network. Maintain an explicit procedure for forgotten badges, missing attendees, last-minute registrations and repeat entry. Odoo speeds up validation; it does not replace the business decision in exception cases.

Connect arrival to commercial follow-up, carefully

Attendance can enrich campaign, lead or post-event activity, but it is not proof of purchase intent on its own. In Odoo Enterprise, define what should trigger follow-up: attending a demonstration, requesting a meeting, stated interest or simply attendance. Teams working in Belgium and France benefit from one shared rule while adapting communications to local language and context.

Test the complete welcome scenario

Underside fictional scenario: an SME runs a customer morning in Brussels and an equivalent session in Lille. It tests badges with fictional attendees, finds a registrant by email, handles a missing name and confirms that checked-in attendance appears on the correct event. The owner approves opening only after checking welcome rights, hardware and the follow-up flow.

  • Verify a scanned badge, a name search and an email search.
  • Check access rights for every person working at the registration desk.
  • Test exception cases: forgotten badge, duplicate, unregistered attendee and late arrival.
  • Confirm that the attendance status appears in the intended attendee report.
  • Define the CRM follow-up rule and the person responsible for applying it.
  • Review French and English communications separately before the event.
