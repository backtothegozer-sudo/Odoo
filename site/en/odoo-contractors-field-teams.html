Problems → answers

Regain control of operations

01 · QUOTING

Complex quotations take too long

Technical variants, supplies, hours, margins and options are scattered across sheets and previous files.

With Odoo: templates, product libraries, variants, sections, options, price rules and approvals make detailed quotes reusable and consistent.

02 · SCHEDULING

The field schedule never stays current

Emergencies, leave, skills, territories and equipment dependencies make static spreadsheets obsolete immediately.

With Odoo: Planning and Field Service provide shared availability and let coordinators reassign work as conditions change.

03 · INFORMATION

Information gets lost between office and field

Plans, instructions, photos and feedback travel through email and chats with no dependable history.

With Odoo: each project or field task brings documents, tasks, messages, photos, time and customer approval together.

04 · MARGIN

Job profitability is hard to measure

Hours, materials, purchases and overruns arrive too late to support decisions.

With Odoo: sales, costs, timesheets, purchasing, inventory and analytic accounting create one view by job.

05 · MATERIALS

Purchasing and inventory are disconnected from jobs

Materials are ordered without demand visibility, reserved manually or consumed without clear allocation.

With Odoo: sales and project demand can drive replenishment, reservations, transfers and traceability.

06 · RECURRENCE

Recurring contracts are underused

Preventive visits and renewals depend on manual reminders, creating missed service and revenue.

With Odoo: Subscriptions, Helpdesk and Field Service organise maintenance cycles, commitments, reminders and recurring billing.

Frequently asked questions

Odoo for contractors

Is Odoo suitable for a construction company?

Yes. Odoo can connect CRM, quotations, projects, job sites, purchasing, inventory, time, invoicing and accounting. The right scope depends on your processes and field constraints.

How can Odoo manage field teams?

Field Service, Planning, Project, Timesheets and Inventory can schedule technicians, share job information, track time and materials, attach photos and collect customer approval.

Can Odoo track job profitability?

Yes. Sales, purchases, inventory usage, timesheets, subcontracting and invoicing can feed a consistent analytic structure for each job.

Can Odoo replace an existing industry-specific system?

Sometimes. An assessment identifies what should be replaced, retained or integrated. The objective is to improve the whole flow, not migrate for its own sake.

Can Odoo connect to accounting or industry-specific software?

Yes, when a reliable API, exchange format or other interface is available. We define master data, ownership and error handling.

How much does an Odoo project cost for a contracting business?

Cost depends on scope, users, migration, integrations, adaptations and rollout. A sound estimate follows an assessment; we do not invent a generic price.
