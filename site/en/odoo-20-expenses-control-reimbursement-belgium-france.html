Odoo 20 Expenses: control and reimburse without losing receipts

A reliable expense claim is more than a photo of a receipt: it must be connected to the right person, account, project and reimbursement. For an Odoo company in Belgium or France, Odoo 20 Expenses provides the flow from expense to payment. Quality then depends on the categories, permissions and controls set by the organisation.

Start with categories that carry the rules

Define categories for expenses that are actually permitted: travel, meals, mileage, accommodation or one-off purchases. A category can hold a unit price, taxes and an expense account. Do not leave employees to choose a vague posting: a clear rule at entry reduces corrections at closing time.

Employees record the amount, date, employee and payer. Choosing Employee prepares a reimbursement; choosing Company distinguishes an expense already paid by the organisation. When a cost must be reinvoiced, connect it to the customer sales order before approval and check the analytic distribution.

Use OCR as assisted control, not as evidence

Odoo 20 can create an expense from an uploaded or drag-and-dropped receipt when expense digitisation is enabled. Settings allow on-demand or automatic digitisation, and the service uses IAP credits. Extraction speeds up entry, but the amount, tax, category and receipt still need review.

An email alias can also create an expense from a received receipt. Odoo accepts only authenticated employee work emails for this route: retain that restriction and test addresses before sharing the alias with teams.

Keep submission, approval, posting and payment separate

A draft expense is not yet a promise to reimburse. The employee submits the lines; users with the required permissions approve or refuse them with a reason. Finance then posts the journal entries. Only posted expenses can be reimbursed from the relevant journal, for example through a bank transfer.

Acceptance-test the scenario people really use

Fictional Underside scenario: a team split between Brussels and Lille records mileage, a hotel stay and a meal linked to a customer assignment. It checks the category, receipt, analytic distribution, approval path, journal entry and payment.

  • Define applicable categories, accounts, taxes and internal policies.
  • Test manual entry, a receipt attachment and a digitised receipt.
  • Verify employee, approver and accountant rights with real test accounts.
  • Check analytic allocation and customer reinvoicing before approval.
  • Test a reasoned refusal, correction and resubmission.
  • Reconcile a paid reimbursement with its journal entry and original receipt.

Official source and scope

Odoo 20’s official documentation covers logging expenses, processing them and reimbursement. The control, permission and acceptance-testing recommendations here are Underside analysis to adapt to your policies, accounting and Odoo rollout in Belgium or France.
