Odoo 19 Email Templates: personalise without losing control

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, an email template is more than a time-saver. In Odoo 19 Enterprise, it connects a message to a document, recipients and a language. That automation is useful only when its copy, dynamic fields and approval process remain controlled.

Start with one precise business use

Create a template for a clearly identified situation: sending a quotation, following up an invoice, confirming an appointment or progressing an opportunity. A template that is too generic makes users rewrite every message; one that is too specialised becomes unmaintainable. Give each template an owner, an Odoo document and a clear point at which sending is allowed.

Personalise with dependable data

Dynamic placeholders can insert a customer name, amount, due date or record-specific link. Test them with realistic cases, including missing data. Do not hide uncertain data behind automated wording: provide a fallback value or prevent sending until the required field is completed.

Keep French and English genuinely separate

In Belgium and France, the customer language should control the delivered rendering. Check the subject line, call to action, greetings, dates, amounts and links separately. Literal translation is not enough: the French and English messages should each be reviewed in their final form, with dynamic data visible.

Govern senders and replies

A transactional message must come from the expected address and let the recipient reply to the right person. Define the sender, reply-to address and recipients before launching a bulk send. Editing rights should remain limited to people who understand the customer journey and the consequences of an incorrect send.

Test before bulk delivery

Underside fictional scenario: an SME prepares quotation templates for Brussels and Lille. It creates two test contacts, checks the French and English versions, reviews a discounted quotation and one with an incomplete address, then sends a preview to the sales team. The template is released only after copy, links and reply address have been approved.

  • Associate each template with a business use, a document and an owner.
  • Test dynamic placeholders with both complete and incomplete data.
  • Approve the French and English versions separately, including subject line and links.
  • Check sender, reply-to address and recipients.
  • Preview desktop and mobile rendering before a bulk send.
  • Keep an approval process for sensitive changes.
