Odoo 19 Field Service Worksheets: standardise field reports without losing detail

For an Odoo business in Belgium or France, a field visit should produce more than a completed status. Odoo 19 Enterprise field service worksheets give technicians a shared structure for instructions, checks, readings and attachments. Their value nevertheless relies on the right template and on how the team verifies the captured information.

Start with useful evidence, not a generic form

Begin with what the customer, operations team or billing process must be able to confirm: equipment reference, completed check, recorded reading, fault, photographs or client decision. A template for each intervention type avoids asking the same irrelevant questions on every visit and makes reports comparable.

Configure the template in the right place

In Field Service, enable Worksheets in Settings. Odoo then installs Studio, where templates can use fields, checkboxes, text, HTML, files, images and links. A template can be selected directly on a task; a project can also define the default template for its new field-service tasks.

Use required fields deliberately

A required field prevents saving until it is filled in. Keep that rule for information without which the report cannot be used: serial number, safety outcome or customer decision. Odoo changes the smart-button label to “Worksheet Complete” as soon as a worksheet is saved, so the label does not prove that every optional field contains data.

Acceptance-test the mobile journey and exceptions

Underside fictional scenario: a maintenance company operating in Belgium and northern France creates two templates, one for preventive visits and one for repairs. It tests a task with each template, checks required fields, attaches a photograph and verifies that a technician can record a fault without bypassing the process.

  • Link every question to an expected decision or piece of evidence.
  • Create one template per intervention family without multiplying variants.
  • Make only genuinely blocking data required.
  • Test the template on a pilot task and on devices used in the field.
  • Define a clear route for faults and attachments.

Official source and scope

Odoo 19 documentation on worksheets explains activation, template design in Studio and use on a task. The governance and acceptance-testing recommendations are from Underside.
